## 📝 중학 핵심 영단어 20선 체크리스트

### Part 1 (1~10)

- [ ] **export** [엑스포트] 수출하다, 수출 — "The country bans the export of AI chips."
- [ ] **scandal** [스캔들] 스캔들, 추문 — "The scandal caused big trouble in the company."
- [ ] **delay** [딜레이] 미루다, 지연시키다 — "Don't delay your decision."
- [ ] **department** [디파트먼트] 부서, 정부 부처 — "She works at the commerce department."
- [ ] **disrupt** [디스럽트] 지장을 주다, 방해하다 — "A power outage can disrupt daily life."
- [ ] **incident** [인시던트] 사건, 사고 — "The company called the outage a technical incident."
- [ ] **restore** [리스토어] 복구하다, 회복시키다 — "They worked hard to restore the network."
- [ ] **apologize** [어폴로자이즈] 사과하다 — "The CEO apologized for the mistake."
- [ ] **negotiate** [니고시에이트] 협상하다 — "Both sides sat down to negotiate peace."
- [ ] **conflict** [콘플릭트] 갈등, 충돌 — "They want to end the long conflict."

### Part 2 (11~20)

- [ ] **diplomatic** [디플로매틱] 외교적인 — "The two countries broke diplomatic ties."
- [ ] **limit** [리밋] 제한하다, 한계 — "There is a speed limit on the internet."
- [ ] **victory** [빅토리] 승리 — "They celebrated their victory."
- [ ] **pressure** [프레셔] 압박, 압력 — "Economic pressure can change a nation's policy."
- [ ] **status** [스테이터스] 상태, 지위 — "They agreed to keep the status quo."
- [ ] **settle** [세틀] 해결하다, 합의를 보다 — "They tried to settle the argument quietly."
- [ ] **asylum** [어사일럼] 망명 — "Many people applied for asylum at the border."
- [ ] **suspend** [서스펜드] 중단하다, 유예하다 — "The court decided to suspend the new order."
- [ ] **decision** [디시전] 결정, 판결 — "The judge will make a final decision."
- [ ] **overturn** [오버턴] 뒤집다, 파기하다 — "The higher court overturned the ruling."
